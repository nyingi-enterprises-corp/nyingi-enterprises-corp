# Executive Summary:

This plan is designed to support the foundation and initial growth of a small dairy cow business near Nyeri, Kenya. The main purpose of this business will be to serve as an investment and additional source of income for the ------------ family. 

Initial investment in the business and well as startup expenses will be invested by members of the family currently residing in the United States. These investments will be made in exchange for a controlling stake in the business. The family members in the United States anticipate initial reinvestment into the business of any profits or dividends with potential extraction of returns after the business is well established.

Business operations will be mainly handled by members of the family residing near Nyeri. It is anticipated that the main person in charge of operations will be ------------- ---------, with additional help from other family members, as needed. Additionally, the business may contract with locally sourced temporary laborers, outside consultants, and vendors, as needed. 

The business will initially start with 2-3 cows and be run from facilities constructed on land owned by a senior member of the family. This operational base is a small portion of the land that houses the senior member's personal property (i.e. a backyard), so total space will be initially limited. The housing for the dairy cows (barn) will be a barn with a cement floor and a metal roof.The business will contract with local vendors to supply necessary consumption items for the cows to produce milk, including feed, medical care, cleaning supplies, breeding services, and logistics. 

Anticipated revenue streams for the business include milk produced by the cows, male calfs excess female calfs, and cow manure fertilizer. 

Anticipated costs for the business include initial cow procurement, cow medical care, cleaning supplies, breeding services, and logistics, fees and taxes, and manual labor for business operations.

The business anticipates the following timeline:

- 0-6 months: Establishment and proof-of-concept phase
- 6-12 months: Refinement of initial operational method
- 12-36 months: Scaling and optimization
- 36 months +: Self sustainment
